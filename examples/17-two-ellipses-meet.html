Curved quads · step 23 · test of the blend

Two Ellipses Meet

  1. Two ellipses per pixel corner. The row knife through the corner marks two edges; the marks inherit their normals; they give one ellipse across the quad. The column knife does the same and gives a second ellipse.
  2. Both pass through the corner's line of sight. Where each passes it is one quadratic.
  3. The corner is a blend of the two. Each ellipse is exactly right at its own two marks, so it counts most near them: the row ellipse counts by how far the corner is from the column ellipse's marks, and the other way round. In the middle they count equally. The same blend gives the point and its u, v.
  4. Where only one ellipse exists (a face bulging past its own edges, where the other knife has nothing to mark), that one is used.
  5. Facing away: a point whose inherited normal faces away from the camera is dropped.
