Curved quads · pixel grid as knives

Pixel Slices

  1. Every pixel edge on the screen is a straight line. Seen from the camera, a straight line is a flat plane: a knife.
  2. Knives (3D): the selected pixel's four knives cut the patch. Blue knives are the pixel's top and bottom edges, orange knives its left and right edges. The colored curves are the cuts.
  3. Patch (u, v) square: every knife's cut drawn on the patch. The light strip is everything the selected pixel's row owns. The dark piece is what the pixel owns.
  4. Dots: where a blue cut meets an orange cut. That's a pixel corner on the patch. Switch the surface to see where finding that corner has a closed form.
