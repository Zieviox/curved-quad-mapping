Quad Pieces

  1. Same math as step 9 (handles, arcs, fillets, the fit, the plane-pencil mapping); the conversion around it is rewritten from scratch.
  2. Each quad is converted on its own: lookups → vertex rounding → arcs (one shared cache, so every piece that uses an arc gets the identical curve) → pieces → splits → surfaces.
  3. Splits: first at artificial handles (S and twist). Then further, cutting a piece in half across the middles of two opposite sides, until its border arcs lie on its own quadric, or the split depth limit is reached.
  4. A piece whose corner keeps two arcs that can't share one quadric (two-point rule) can only get smaller, not exact. The table at the bottom shows how the crack shrinks with depth.
