Pixel Corners

  1. The pieces come from step 10 unchanged (handles, arcs, rounded sharp edges, one quadric per piece, split depth).
  2. Every pixel corner is a ray from the camera. For each piece whose screen box contains the corner: ray vs the piece's quadric (one quadratic), then its 4 side planes give (u, v) and whether the hit is inside the piece. The nearest hit wins.
  3. Each pixel then owns the (u, v) of its 4 corners. Neighbouring pixels share corners, so this costs about one corner per pixel.
  4. Reference: the same pieces projected densely. A corner inside the reference footprint that hits nothing is a hole (a crack or hole seen by the pixels). A footprint pixel with no corner hit is a gap.
