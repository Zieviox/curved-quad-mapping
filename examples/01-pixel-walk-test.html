Pixel Walk Test

Walks every curved quad in (u, v) steps sized to one projected pixel and emits (quad, u, v, x, y). A dense resample of the same surface is the reference footprint. A gap is a pixel inside a quad's footprint where that quad emitted no pair. Tap any pixel to inspect it; drag to orbit the camera.

Safety scales du and dv (1.0 = exactly one pixel per step, as in the handoff). Max step caps du and dv in parameter units; it matters near silhouettes, where the screen speed drops and the step grows. Edge snap forces a last sample at u = 1 and a last row at v = 1.

Area is measured on a 4 × 4 sub-pixel grid from the reference samples. Sliver: the quad covers 2/16 of the pixel or less. The other classes use the reference sample nearest the pixel center. Border: within 1.5 px of a patch edge. Silhouette: surface within ~11.5° of edge-on (|cos| < 0.2). Interior: everything else, meaning a step or row spacing skipped the pixel.

Timing

StagemsNote

The walk is warmed up for 80 ms, then timed by re-running it for at least 60 ms. This is JavaScript (float64) on this device, so ns per step is not comparable with the Skylake cycle table (Phong/Nagata 26 or 36, PN-quad 32 or 40 cycles). It is useful for comparing settings on the same phone.
